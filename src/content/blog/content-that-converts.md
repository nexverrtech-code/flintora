Plenty of content ranks and still does nothing for the business. Traffic goes up, enquiries stay flat. When that happens, the content is usually missing one of these five things.

## 1. It targets a buyer, not a keyword

A keyword tells you what someone typed. It does not tell you who they are or what they need next. Content that converts starts with a specific reader and a specific problem, then uses keywords to reach them.

## 2. It answers the question quickly

Readers decide in seconds whether a page will help. Put the answer near the top, then use the rest of the page to explain, prove and expand.

## 3. It shows proof

Claims are cheap. Examples, numbers, screenshots, quotes and process details show that you have done this before. One specific example beats five general statements.

## 4. It makes the next step obvious

Every piece should lead somewhere: a related guide, a template, a demo, a call. Match the ask to the reader's stage. Someone reading a beginner guide is rarely ready for a sales call, but may happily download a checklist.

## 5. It gets updated

Content ages. Prices change, tools change and competitors publish. Pages that are reviewed and refreshed keep their rankings and their credibility.

## A quick audit you can run today

Open your ten most visited articles and ask of each one:

- Who is this for?
- Does it answer the question in the first screen?
- Where is the proof?
- What should the reader do next?
- When was it last updated?

Fixing those five things on pages that already have traffic is often the fastest content win available.
